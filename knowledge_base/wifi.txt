Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

If you are unable to connect to Wi-Fi:

1. Check that Wi-Fi is enabled on your device.
2. Make sure you are connected to the correct network.
3. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
4. Restart your device.
5. Forget the Wi-Fi network and reconnect using the correct password.
6. Check whether other devices can connect to the same network.
7. If no devices can connect, the network or router may be experiencing an issue.

If the problem continues after these steps, contact the support team.
